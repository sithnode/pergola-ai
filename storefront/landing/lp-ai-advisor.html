We have a 12×14 concrete patio. HOA says no wood, must match house color (gray/charcoal). Budget around $3,000.

Got it — your HOA restricts wood, so our Modern Aluminum 12×16 ($2,499) is a great fit. It's available in Charcoal Gray, rust-proof, and comes with a 10-year warranty.
Will it actually fit a 12×14 patio? Won't it hang over?
🌿
Good question. The 12×16 means the posts sit at 12×16 — the rafters extend slightly beyond. On a 12×14 patio the 2 ft overhang on each end looks intentional and provides extra shade. Most customers love it. I'd actually recommend it over the smaller model for your space.
Do I need a permit for this?
🌿
For a freestanding pergola under 200 sq ft, most jurisdictions don't require a permit. A 12×16 = 192 sq ft, so you're likely just under the threshold. Check with your local building dept to confirm — they're usually helpful for quick questions like this.
